# Provisional pilot fixture

All size labels are assistant-authored `demo_ordinal` ranks. They are not human ratings, sourced measurements, or a validated instrument. There were no participants or reliability estimates. `make_fixture.py` makes the authorship and exact values inspectable and regenerates the CSV files.

Size means approximate overall spatial extent of an ordinary full-sized physical object. Elongated, flat and bulky objects are only placed in coarse demonstration ranks; no single physical metric or equal spacing is asserted. Context transformations refer to a particular described subject and do not change the ordinary-category labels.

Six categories have ten concepts each. Their overlapping ranks do not eliminate confounding: transport occupies mostly large ranks and produce mostly small ranks. All categories contain several ordinal levels. Some cases remain interpretation-dependent (e.g. folded versus extended ladder, instrument models); the fixture is intended to expose engineering behavior and must be replaced or independently validated for dissertation evidence.

The comparison-pair CSV is separately authored qualitative demonstration data. A grain of table salt and a forty-foot shipping container are reference-only objects; they are excluded from probes and substantially reused. Pair assignments use clearly separated typical sizes. Behavioral inference is conditional on these two references.

No per-rater observations are included because none exist. Use `qd import-data` to preserve independent labels, provenance, units, aggregation and optional uncertainty, with an optional separate per-rater table.
